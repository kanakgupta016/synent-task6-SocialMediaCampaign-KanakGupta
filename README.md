1. Objective : 

To create a powerful social media campaign for Bacca Bucci shoes by combining modern sneaker culture with ancient Egyptian aesthetics to showcase the brand as bold, legendary, and stylish.


2. Steps Performed : 

  a. Planned a luxury and cinematic visual theme
  b. Used Egyptian-inspired backgrounds and elements
  c. Added bold typography and strong taglines
  d. Highlighted shoe designs with dramatic lighting and composition
  e. Maintained a premium and consistent brand look across all posts

3. Tools Used : 

  a. Adobe Photoshop
  b. Adobe Illustrator
  c. PNG Assets & Mockup Elements

4. Outcome :

The final campaign creates a strong and premium visual identity for Bacca Bucci, making the products look powerful, fashionable, and memorable for social media marketing.
